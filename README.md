### 🔬 About me

I'm a B.Tech. UG student, working where **power systems meet computing**. My research models how cloud and AI data centers draw power, using stochastic workload models, component-level load models and power-system simulation, and what those loads mean for grid stability. I also work on **multi-agent systems**: stochastic models of multi-agent LLM workflows and safe multi-agent reinforcement learning.

- 🎓 **Bachelor's thesis:** *Dynamic Load Modeling and Stability Analysis of High-Density Computational Infrastructure*
- ➗ Mathematics enthusiast, focused on stochastic modelling.
- 🧠 **Research interests:** stochastic modeling, energy-efficient computing, safe multi-agent reinforcement learning.
- 🛠️ **Tools:** Python, MATLAB/Simulink, TensorFlow, LaTeX.
- 🌱 Open to research collaborations, internships and graduate opportunities.
